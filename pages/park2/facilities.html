FACILITY STUDY

Observed & Reported Facilities

The project materials support the following observations. No unsupported facility counts have been added.

01

Green Area

Green space is the most-used area according to the survey, selected by 40% of respondents.

02

Paths & Access

The field photographs show paved/arched pathways connecting different parts of the park.

03

Play / Recreation Area

Photographs show recreational/play equipment and an open activity area.

04

Waste Bins / Cleanliness

The survey identifies lack of dustbins as the main problem (35%) and better cleanliness as an important improvement (40%).

05

Toilets / Amenities

Toilets are the most selected facility improvement request, chosen by 30% of respondents.

06

Seating & Other Facilities

The survey records average facility satisfaction (45%), indicating scope for facility upgrades.
