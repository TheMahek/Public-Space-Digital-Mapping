ABOUT THE PROJECT

Understanding
Our Public Spaces

A field-based study exploring how people use public spaces and how these spaces can be better understood through observation, surveys and digital mapping.

INTRODUCTION

What Is This Project About?

Public spaces such as parks, gardens and playgrounds are important parts of community life. They provide places for people to relax, exercise, interact and spend time outdoors.

Our project studies selected public spaces to understand how they are actually used by different groups of people.

Through field observation and surveys, we collect information about activities, facilities, usage patterns and areas that may need improvement.

Our Focus

People, activities, facilities, usage patterns and problem areas within selected public spaces.

OBJECTIVES

What We Aim to Study

FIELD STUDY

What We Look At

User Groups

Who uses the space and how different groups interact with it.

Activities

Walking, exercising, playing, sitting and social activities.

Facilities

Benches, tracks, playgrounds, lighting, dustbins and other facilities.

Usage Patterns

Areas that are highly used, moderately used or rarely used.

PROJECT OUTCOME

From Observation
to Understanding

The information collected during our field study helps us understand how public spaces are being used and where improvements may be needed.

EXPLORE PARKS

CONCLUSION

Understanding Our Public Spaces

This project helped us understand how people use public spaces and how different facilities influence their experience. Through field observations, surveys, and digital mapping, we aim to identify commonly used areas, available facilities, and problems that may need attention.

The findings from our selected parks can help highlight areas for improvement and encourage the development of public spaces that are more accessible, comfortable, safe, and user-friendly.
