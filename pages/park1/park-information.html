PUBLIC SPACE USAGE

Usage & Study Highlights

These points are based on the supplied survey summary and the field photographs included with the project.

Walking / Jogging

Walking and jogging are the most selected main purposes in the survey, chosen by 35% of respondents.

Resting / Seating

Seating is identified as the main problem by 35% of respondents, indicating demand for more convenient resting spaces.

Recreation

Photographs show landscaped open areas and a children’s recreational/play area within the park.

Green Space

The field photographs show substantial greenery, planted areas and shaded sections.
