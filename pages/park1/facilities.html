FACILITY STUDY

Observed & Reported Facilities

The project materials support the following observations. No unsupported facility counts have been added.

01

Walking Paths

Paved walking routes are visible in the field photographs and are central to the park’s use.

02

Seating Areas

Benches and resting areas are visible, while the survey identifies lack of seating as the main problem (35%).

03

Play / Recreation Area

Photographs show a children’s play and recreation area used within the park.

04

Waste Management

Waste/litter is visible in some photographed areas and waste management is identified as a concern in the study.

05

Lighting

A lighting fixture is visible in the field photographs; 35% selected better lighting as an important improvement.

06

Green & Open Areas

Landscaped lawns, trees and planted borders form a significant part of the public space.
