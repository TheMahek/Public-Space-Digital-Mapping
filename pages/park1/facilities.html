Park Facilities

An overview of the facilities available within the selected public space.

FACILITY STUDY

Available Facilities

The following facilities are included for observation during the field study.

01

Seating Areas

Approximately 8 benches are available across the park, mainly near walking areas and open spaces.

02

Walking Paths

The park has a paved walking path used mainly by morning walkers and evening visitors.

03

Play Area

A small children's play area is available with basic recreational equipment.

04

Waste Bins

Around 5 waste bins are placed at different points, although some areas have limited coverage.

05

Lighting

Streetlights and park lights provide illumination, particularly around the main walking path.

06

Drinking Water

One drinking-water point is available near the main entrance of the park.
