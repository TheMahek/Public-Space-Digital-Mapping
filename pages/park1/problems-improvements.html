PARK 01 • FIELD ASSESSMENT

Problems & Improvements

Issues identified from the supplied photographs and visitor survey, followed by practical improvement suggestions.

FINDINGS

Areas That Need Attention

The findings below distinguish visible field evidence from survey-reported concerns.

01
IDENTIFIED ISSUE

Damaged / Uneven Paving

A field photograph shows a visibly damaged section of paved path. This can make movement less comfortable and indicates a maintenance requirement.

Why it mattersAddressing this issue can improve comfort, cleanliness, accessibility or the overall public-space experience.
SUGGESTED IMPROVEMENT

Practical Action

Repair and level damaged paving sections and inspect paths regularly.

02
IDENTIFIED ISSUE

Waste & Litter

Photographs show litter in a planted area. The survey also identifies waste management among the concerns reported by visitors.

Why it mattersAddressing this issue can improve comfort, cleanliness, accessibility or the overall public-space experience.
SUGGESTED IMPROVEMENT

Practical Action

Improve waste-bin coverage and regular collection/maintenance.

03
IDENTIFIED ISSUE

Limited Seating

Lack of seating is the most selected main problem, reported by 35% of respondents.

Why it mattersAddressing this issue can improve comfort, cleanliness, accessibility or the overall public-space experience.
SUGGESTED IMPROVEMENT

Practical Action

Add or reposition benches near walking paths, shaded areas and frequently used sections.

04
IDENTIFIED ISSUE

Lighting & Maintenance

Better lighting is the most selected important improvement (35%), while better maintenance is the most selected future improvement (40%).

Why it mattersAddressing this issue can improve comfort, cleanliness, accessibility or the overall public-space experience.
SUGGESTED IMPROVEMENT

Practical Action

Review lighting coverage and strengthen routine maintenance of public-space facilities.

What Visitors Asked to Improve

SURVEY HIGHLIGHT

CONCLUSION

Connecting Evidence to Improvement

The field photographs show the physical character and visible issues of the space, while the 20-response survey identifies what visitors most frequently selected as concerns and improvement needs. Together, these findings provide a basis for practical park improvements.
